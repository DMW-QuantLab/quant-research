## Strategy thesis

Probability mass leakage: on Polymarket mutually-exclusive range markets (currently tweet-count, Culture category), when Σ(ask prices) across a feasibility-filtered bucket subset falls materially below 1.0, buying that subset guarantees payout = stake/Σp regardless of which bucket resolves. Formalized as Range Sniping Arbitrage (RSA), investor memo v1.1 (Feb 2026): target 15-35% gross annualized, ~0 market beta.

## Fee model — critical gap, needs full rebuild

- `EXP-001/config.yaml` currently pins `taker_fee_bps: 2` (0.02%) — stale
- Verified live (2026-09-19): Polymarket Fee Structure V2, effective 2026-03-30, still current. Category-based taker fees; Culture/Economics/Weather/Other at 5%, formula `fee = shares × rate × price × (1 − price)`. Effective % of notional = `rate × (1 − price)` — cheaper buckets pay a *higher* effective rate, not lower. Makers pay 0% + earn rebate. Geopolitics/world events remain fee-free.
- `EXP-001/README.md`'s stated breakeven `(1 − 2φ)/(1 + φ) ≈ 0.9608` at φ=2% is internally inconsistent — that formula gives 0.941; 0.9608 is what `(1 − φ)/(1 + φ)` gives. Needs correcting, and since fee is now price-dependent, breakeven needs re-deriving per bucket-subset rather than as one flat threshold.
